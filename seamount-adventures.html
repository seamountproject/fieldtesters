Vallandry, French Alps 26 Sep 2026
Above the valley
45.5°N · 6.7°E · 1800m

Late September in the Tarentaise. The alpine chalets still holding the last of the summer sun, the valley floor already in shadow. At this altitude the temperature drops fast once the light goes — the kind of shift that makes you understand why the animals up here don't wait for it. You either adapt or you descend.

Vallandry · Chapelle de Prariond 26 Sep 2026
The trail to the chapel

A single track worn into dry grass, the kind of path made by people going to the same place for a long time. The larch forest on the slope above, the chapel just coming into view on the ridge. Late afternoon, the light already flattening. The mountain behind had been in shadow for an hour.

Chapelle Notre-Dame de la Vie 26 Sep 2026
The chapel and the ridge

The chapel sits at 1930m on an open shelf of ground, the rock face of the Aiguille Rouge rising behind it. Built in the seventeenth century, surrounded now by dry grass and the smell of larch resin. The cross to the east marks the edge of the flat ground before the slope drops away into the valley. You notice how still it is up here when the wind stops.
